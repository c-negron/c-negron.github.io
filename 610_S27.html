-- Topics covered
The plan is to cover some basic homological algebra, then move into a discussion of derived categories and infty-categories.
Part I Homological algebra: Ext and Tor, cochains and quasi-isomorphism, spectral sequences. We should follow basic examples of (quasi-coherent) sheaves on varieties, modules over algebras, and algebraic group representations.
Part II Derived categories and infty-categories: Classical constructions of derived categories and derived functors. Compact objects and compact generation. Simplicial sets and (synthetic) infinity-categories. Whatever we can say about infty-categories.
